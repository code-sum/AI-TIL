### [ICLR 2025] SCoRe: Training Language Models to Self-Correct via Reinforcement Learning [🗂️](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12917)

- 3줄 요약
  - dd

- Motivation
  - There are many attempts to solve math and coding problems using LLM models
  - However, for various problems, LLM does not always present only the correct answers
  - Generally, to train the model, the correct answer is directly informed or seperate model is used to give feedback
  - However, what we want is not just to find answer, but LLM's ability to detect and fix mistakes or errors on its own
 
- Problem
  - When LLM is asked to correct its own mistakes, the model rarely corrects the answers, or turns the answers into incorrect ones
  - In addition, if you are forced to learn the correct answer, LLM will only learn how to guess the correct answer, not the "correction process."
