### [SOSP 2025] KTransformers: Unleashing the Full Potential of CPU/GPU Hybrid Inference for MoE Models [🗂️](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3731569.3764843)

- 3줄 요약
  - MoE 모델은 CPU/GPU hybrid inference에 적합하지만, 기존 시스템은 CPU 연산 한계와 동기화 overhead로 인해 DeepSeek-V3/R1 같은 대규모 모델 구동 시 병목이 발생
  - 이를 해결하기 위해 제안된 KTransformers는 AMX-specialized kernel과 비동기 태스크 스케줄링을 도입하여 prefilling 속도를 4.62–19.74배, decoding 속도를 1.25–4.09배 대폭 향상
  - 아울러 새로운 Expert Deferral 메커니즘으로 CPU 활용률을 거의 100%까지 끌어올려 정확도 손실 없이 처리량을 최대 1.45배 추가 개선함으로써, 로컬 환경에서의 대형 MoE 모델 접근성을 크게 높임

- Introduction
  - MoE (Mixture-of-Experts) LLMs have demonstrated impressive performance across diverse tasks
    - Activates only a subset of experts within each FFN layer
    - Significantly reduces compute demands
  - MoE
    - Experts: subset of specialized subnetworks at each layer for each token
    - Dense FFN is replaced with a large pool of expert networks
    - A learned gating mechanism dynamically selects the most relevant experts to process each token

- KTransformers: a system that enables efficient local inference for large MoE models on hybrid CPU/GPU platforms
  - Proposed Techniques:
    - AMX-optimized kernels
    - CPU-GPU coordination
    - Expert Deferral strategy
  - It maximizes HW utilization and significantly improves throughput by 1.66-19.74x
