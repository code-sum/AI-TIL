### [ACL 2025] Are Rules Meant to be Broken? Understanding Multilingual Moral Reasoning as a Computational Pipeline with UniMoral [🗂️](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.294/)

- 3줄 요약
  - UniMoral은 6개 언어(Arabic, Chinese, English, Hindi, Russian, Spanish)의 다양성을 반영하여 action choices, ethical principles, contributing factors, consequences 및 작성자의 moral/cultural profiles를 통합한 한 단계 확장된 moral reasoning 데이터셋
  - 3개 LLMs를 대상으로 action prediction, moral typology classification, factor attribution analysis, consequence generation의 4가지 과제에서 벤치마크 평가를 진행
  - 암묵적으로 포함된 moral contexts가 LLMs의 moral reasoning 능력을 향상시키지만, 성능을 추가로 끌어올리기 위해서는 더욱 specialized approaches가 필요함을 확인

  - Moral Reasoning pipeline
    - An individual encounters a moral scenario, they list out the potential actions then can take, and select one
    - The chosen action yields outcomes affecting stakeholders and societal norms
    - The "Moralsphere" conceptualizes this dynamic interplay between reasoning, action, and societal impact in resolving moral dilemmas

- UNIMORAL Dataset
  - Datacollection
    - (1) Generating scenarios: From three prominent theories
    - (2) Datermining the most probable action options
  - seed scenarios
    - (1) Moral Judgment Interview (MJI): 7
    - (2) the Defining Issues Test (DIT): 7
    - (3) The Moral Competence Test (MCT): 4
  - Analysis
    - (a) Actions have almost equal distributions across languages
    - (b) Ethical preferences in action choices show correlation with annotators' first language, along with global trend
    - (c) Moral decisions are influenced by varing factors, such as emotions and legal frameworks, across languages
    - (d) Moral and cultural values vary across languages as revealed by MFQ2 and VSM assessments
    - (e) The TSNE plot reveals moral and cultural (dis)similarities between languages
