### [ACL 2024] Having Beer after Prayer? Measuring Cultural Bias in Large Language Models [🗂️](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.862.pdf)

- Introduction
  - Having Beer after Prayer?
    - "Maghrib Prayer": Islamic Prayer, Performed in the Evening after the sunset
    - LMs fail Appropriate Cultural Adaptation in Arabic
      - (1) Western: 'Going for a drink' = Alcoholic Beverages
      - (2) Arab: 'Going for a drink' = Coffee or Tea
    - LMs exhibit Bias towards Western Entities
    - LMs should communicate with the awareness of cultural sensitivities
    - No Resource for measuring the Cultural Appropriateness of the LMs > To Measure the Cultural Bias of the LMs

- Discussion
  - Key Takeaways
    - (1) Objective Data Construction: Dataset designed without LM involvement (Wikidata and web crawling), with manual annotation and review
    - (2) Reflecting Real-World Language Contexts: Evaluation conducted using natural Twitter/X sentences as masked prompts
    - (3) Tracing Root Causes: Analyzing CBS across different Arabic pretraining corpora to identify sources of data dias
    - (4) Social Implications: Insights into stereotypes and distortions that language models may impose on specific culture
