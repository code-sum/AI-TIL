### [ACL 2025] From REAL to SYNTHETIC: Synthesizing Millions of Diversified and Complicated User Instructions with Attributed Grounding [🗂️](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.517/)

- 3줄 요약
  - 다양하고 복잡한 대규모 instruction data는 LLM의 자동 alignment에 필수적이지만, 기존 synthetic instruction 방식은 grounding 출처의 한계나 단순 확장 문제로 인해 고품질 데이터 확보에 어려움
  - 본 연구는 이를 해결하기 위해 real instruction을 사용자 맥락과 연결하는 top-down attribution과 웹 문서 기반으로 상황과 지시어를 생성하는 bottom-up synthesis를 포함한 attributed grounding 프레임워크를 제안
  - 이 프레임워크로 구축한 100만 개 규모의 SynthQuestions dataset으로 학습한 모델은 주요 benchmark에서 뛰어난 성능을 달성했으며, web corpora가 확장됨에 따라 성능이 지속적으로 scaling됨을 입증

- Alignment
  - Training large language models to understand human intents.
  - Many works have put effort into collecting SFT data from humans. But human annotation is expensive, which leads to a limited scale and scope.

- Instruction Data Synthesizing
  - As the generating ability of LLMs become stronger, more recent works seek to synthesize SFT data automatically to break through the scale limit of human annotating.
  - Self-Instruction(2023, Wang et al), Alpaca, WizardLM(2024, Xu et al)

- Motivation
  - Diverse, complex, and large-scale instruction data is crucial for automatically aligning LLMs
  - Prior works suffer from limited grounding sources, leading to narrow distribution, or rely on trivial extensions that fail to produce meaningful trajectories in terms of complexity

- Researh Overview
  - Propose a synthesizing framework based on the core idea of attributed grounding
    - (1) Make a seed dataset RealQuestions
    - (2) Based on the RealQuestions dataset, synthesize SynthQuestions
