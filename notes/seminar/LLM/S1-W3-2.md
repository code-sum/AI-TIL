### [EMNLP 2024] Fool Me Once? Contrasting Textual and Visual Explanations in a Clinical Decision-Support Setting [🗂️](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12284)

- 3줄 요약
  - 본 연구는 흉부 X-ray 분석에서 85명의 의료진을 대상으로 saliency map(시각적 설명), 자연어 설명, 그리고 두 방식을 결합한 세 가지 Explainable AI(XAI) 방식의 실제 효용성을 평가
  - 분석 결과 텍스트 기반 설명은 AI에 대한 과도한 의존을 유발했으나, 이를 saliency map과 결합했을 때 이러한 과의존 문제가 완화되는 것으로 나타남
  - 또한 설명의 사실적 정확성과 AI 자체의 정답 여부 간의 일치도 등 설명의 품질이 각 XAI 방식의 실제 유용성에 중대한 영향을 미침을 확인

- Preliminary
  - Vision Language Model (2023 NeurIPS Oral)
    - Used bounding boxes of entity understanding and complex and conversational data augmentation is done with GPT-4
    - CLIP and additional training for visual representation
  - Saliency Map
    - Weight activation visualized

- Objective of this paper
  - Not introducing a new architecture
  - Want to explain the impact of each output (saliency map, natural language explanation, classification) in medical vlm evaluation

- Experimental Setting
  - Models
    - DenseNet-based VLM
    - Grad-CAM saliency map
    - each part of used to consists the four models before
    - MIMIC-NLE > chest X-ray vlm dataset
  - Human Evaluation
    - C_AI represents the classification correctness: 0 or 1
    - C_x represents the validity of the explanation: 1 to 7 scale
    - 80 clinician involved
    - GT is given by expert clinicians

- Results
  - Comparing GT from experts and test data through GLMM
    - did they accept the right results and detect the wrong results well?

- Insights
  - (1) Medical VLM is evaluated with overreliance on NLE
  - (2) Alignment is important (even when they are wrong)
  - (3) Saliency map is helpful only when they are aligned

- Key takeaways
  - (1) For AI product, this kind of research is more critical (benchmarks are not reliable often in applications)
  - (2) Conclusion of the papar: people tend to rely on more complex looking information whether it is correct or not but AI does not
  - (3) Title is also important: Fool Me Once

- Weakness
  - More recent models could be used. Also, if a technical solution were included, the paper would be far better
